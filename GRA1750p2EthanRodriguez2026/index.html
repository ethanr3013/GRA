Spaghetti and Meatballs

A delicious meal with meatballs, noodles, and marinara sauce.

Ingredients

  • Ground Meat
  • Egg and Water
  • Breadcrumbs and Cheese
  • Marinara Sauce
  • Spaghetti

Steps

  1. Start the meatball mixture.
  2. Mix in the meat, breadcrumbs, and cheese.
  3. Form the meatballs.
  4. Bake.
  5. Simmer the meatballs.
  6. Cook the Spaghetti.
  7. Toss and serve.
